## Strategic frame (drives every item below)

1. **Coach-first tiering.** Tier 1 = coaches (nutritionists + trainers) — all robustness,
   premium AI, monetization land here. Tier 2/3 = trainee/client — connected but NOT the
   primary monetization target and NOT adversarial. Biggest entity = the gym/organization
   (holds both nutritionists and trainers).
2. **AI must EMPOWER the coach, never undercut.** "If the trainee learns more from the app
   than from the trainer, you lose the trainer" (B2B churn). The client-side moat is
   **CONTEXT** (per-customer + per-coach AI profile), NOT an open chatbot — clients already
   have Gemini/ChatGPT.
3. **"Grammarly-lock" model for client AI.** Client SEES that insights exist ("AI found N
   things in your profile/form") but must TALK TO THE COACH to unlock the detail. The full
   assessment opens on the COACH side. Empowers coach + app + client together without
   replacing the trainer.
4. **Nutrition = crystallized/honest** (clear answers, no technique to protect).
   **Training = benchmark + technique** (many trainers skip technique). Form-check AI is
   sensitive — keep it a COACH tool; don't embarrass or replace the trainer.
   Progressive-overload benchmarks (squat 100→120kg in 3mo; 400m time ↓) = a fair,
   measurable axis.

## Backlog items

### B1 — Grammarly-lock client AI gating
- **Source:** Michael + Daniel (core strategic decision #3 this meeting).
- **Coach-first framing:** Client surfaces show only a *teaser* ("AI found N things in your
  profile / your form") — the substance is locked behind a coach conversation. The full
  assessment renders on the COACH side. AI becomes a reason to talk to the coach, not a
  substitute for one; protects against B2B churn (decision #2).
- **Priority:** **P0** — this is the gating principle that constrains how every other
  client-facing AI item (B2) ships. Decide it before exposing more AI to clients.
- **Dependencies:** Existing AI client snapshot + one-shot coach insight (P5, shipped).
  Builds on the already-shipped "hide calories/adherence/food-ideas from client view"
  (Call-2 item #3). Needs a teaser-count surface on the client side + a coach-side
  unlock/full-assessment surface.
- **Definition of done:** Client sees an "AI found N insights — talk to your coach to unlock"
  teaser with zero actionable detail; the corresponding full assessment is visible only on
  the coach account for that client.

### B2 — Workout AI form-check + progressive-overload benchmarks
- **Source:** Daniel (WIP w/ Daniela); gated on George's domain input.
- **Coach-first framing:** Pose-tracking dots / form-check stays a COACH tool — surfaced to
  the coach, never used to embarrass or replace the trainer (decision #4). Pairs with
  progressive-overload benchmarks (squat 100→120kg/3mo, 400m time ↓) as a fair, measurable,
  gamified axis the trainee tracks against goals while the technique read-out stays coach-side.
- **Priority:** **P2** — explicitly NOT production yet; needs George's mechanics input first.
- **Dependencies:** MediaPipe Pose form-check (Day 8, shipped to `/dashboard/workout/form-check`)
  + `form_analyses` table; existing workout module (PR detection, volume). **Blocked on**
  the Jun 29 Daniel↔George session (workout mechanics/benchmarks) and George onboarding to the
  workout section. Subject to B1 gating on the client side (form insights = teaser only).
- **Definition of done:** Coach can view a client's form-check analysis + a
  progressive-overload benchmark chart vs goal; client sees only gamified progress, not the
  raw technique critique — and George has signed off on the benchmark definitions.

### B4 — Recipe ingestion → benchmark
- **Source:** Michael (will send recipes + rough macros).
- **Coach-first framing:** Encodes Michael's real prescribed recipes into the food-AI so
  the app "counts for the coach" on the dishes coaches actually use — directly improving the
  accuracy a coach relies on, on their own recipe set.
- **Priority:** **P1** — test ONE recipe end-to-end first, then scale; targets the food-AI
  accuracy goal (90-95%).
- **Dependencies:** Michael delivering recipes + rough macros (external). Reuses
  `recipe-analyze` agent + `dish_recipes` cache + the v3 benchmark harness (median-of-3).
  Feeds the master-plan accuracy track (P7, raise dbResolved → stable 95).
- **Definition of done:** At least one Michael recipe ingested → decomposed → benchmarked
  against his rough macros within tolerance, with a repeatable path to add the rest toward
  90-95% food-AI accuracy.

### B5 — Intake questionnaire tweak with Michael
- **Source:** Michael (clinical interview refinement).
- **Coach-first framing:** Enriches the per-client AI CONTEXT profile (the client-side moat,
  decision #2) with the questions a real nutritionist asks — celebrating-what, typical day,
  sport-habit fit, sleep, energy — so the coach's AI assistant starts from a deeper, more
  human picture than a generic chatbot ever could.
- **Priority:** **P1** — low build cost, high context payoff; schema already exists.
- **Dependencies:** Shipped intake questionnaire (P2) + custom-questionnaire-builder schema
  (migration 0027, Call-2 item #5). Needs Michael's finalized question wording. Feeds the
  per-client/per-coach AI profile that B1's assessments draw on.
- **Definition of done:** New intake questions (celebrating-what / typical day / sport-habit
  fit / sleep / energy) are live in the intake flow and flow through into the AI client
  profile/snapshot.

### B6 — French food accuracy
- **Source:** In-meeting accuracy discussion (food-AI claimed >85%; French lags ~70%).
- **Coach-first framing:** French-speaking coaches can't trust the food-AI until French
  parity matches EN/EL/ES — accuracy is the coach-facing credibility asset, so French
  coaches are blocked from the Tier-1 experience until this closes.
- **Priority:** **P2** — real gap but no French coach in the current partner cohort; needs an
  external French validator before it can be closed.
- **Dependencies:** CIQUAL FR DB (3,323 foods, shipped) + prompt v7 4-language path
  (EN/ES/EL/FR). **Blocked on** recruiting a French-language validator (none on the team).
  Tracks against the master-plan benchmark scale-up (P7).
- **Definition of done:** French food-AI accuracy validated by a native French reviewer and
  raised from ~70% toward EN/EL/ES parity (>85%), with French benchmark cases added to the set.
